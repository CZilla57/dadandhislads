# App ideas / backlog

Parking lot for app concepts not yet on the site. These started as placeholder
entries on the brand site and are kept here as future ideas. Move one into
`src/data/apps.ts` when it's real enough to show.

## The Broken Bone App
- **Tagline:** From the ER to all healed up.
- **Category:** Health
- **Idea:** Track a fracture the whole way through — cast-off dates, follow-ups,
  range-of-motion exercises, and a healing timeline the whole family can follow.
  Born from a real trip to the ER.

## Focus Lads
- **Tagline:** ADHD-friendly focus that actually sticks.
- **Category:** ADHD & Focus
- **Idea:** Body-doubling timers, gentle nudges, and a dopamine-friendly reward
  loop built for brains that wander.

## LedgerLad
- **Tagline:** Small-business books without the headache.
- **Category:** Business
- **Idea:** Invoicing, expenses, and a plain-English snapshot of how the business
  is really doing — for makers, freelancers, and side-hustlers.

## Chore Quest
- **Tagline:** Turn the family to-do list into a game.
- **Category:** Family
- **Idea:** Kids earn points, level up, and unlock rewards for helping out.

## Brain Break
- **Tagline:** Tiny games for a big reset.
- **Category:** Fun
- **Idea:** Quick, delightful mini-games to reset an overloaded brain.
